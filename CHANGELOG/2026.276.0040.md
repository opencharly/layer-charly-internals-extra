# 2026.276.0040 — docs(internals-extra): retire the schema-version claims from the skill entities

## Summary

The `internals-extra` family still described the authored per-entity `version:` as MANDATORY. The schema-versioning removal cutover (`charly#749`/`#754`) deleted it, and the candy schema is CLOSED, so a candy carrying `version:` now fails closed-CUE at load. - `spec/spec/version_gen.go` is **absent**; `spec/internal/schemagen` has three modes (`concat | vocab | retag`), not four.
